## How to Reduce IPTV Buffering on Android TV

Buffering is usually where the fun ends and everyone starts staring at the router.

TiviLive recommends **25+ Mbps for 4K/UHD streaming** and says Ethernet is preferred when available. Its FAQ also recommends at least 16 Mbps for HD content and says a strong Wi-Fi connection can work.

A sensible setup is therefore:

1. Use Ethernet when your Android TV box supports it.
2. If using Wi-Fi, keep the device within a reasonable range of the router.
3. Test the internet connection directly on the TV or Android TV box rather than assuming your phone gets the same performance.
4. Start with HD playback when troubleshooting instead of immediately blaming everything on 4K.
5. Avoid refreshing large playlists repeatedly when you are trying to diagnose a connection problem.

The important thing is to separate **provider issues** from **home-network issues**. If Netflix, YouTube and other streaming apps are already buffering on the same Android TV device, changing IPTV players may not solve the underlying problem.
